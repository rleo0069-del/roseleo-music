Lyrics
Verse 1
He stands in the shade with the apples still warm
Counting the sheep like they never belonged
To anyone else. The light on her shoulder
Makes the whole valley hold its breath.

Si le pasteur d’Arcadie
Raised the fruit to the sun,
Would he still call it justice
Or just give in to the one?

Chorus
Celle-ci, quoy qu’on l’envie
Mériterait son présent.
Even if the others whisper,
Even if the gods resent.
I’d still place the golden apple
Where her fingers already rest—
Slowly, like a secret.
Like the only thing that matters left.

Verse 2
She walks the long grass like she owns every shadow,
Dress half undone from the heat and the wind.
I watch the way the fabric forgets her,
And I forget every reason I had.

So ick als Paris…
I would step into the judgment too.
Give her the gouwe vrught
And the whole quiet afternoon.

Chorus
Celle-ci, quoy qu’on l’envie
Mériterait son présent.
Let them envy the way she looks

When she knows she has already won.
I’d still place the golden apple

Against the soft of her wrist—
Warm from the sun,
Cool where my mouth just kissed.

Bridge
Minnelijk gelaet…
That soft, almost-smile
When she turns and finds me watching.

The foam-goddess can keep her sea;
I’d rather stay right here,

Learning the sound of her name
In whatever language makes her stay.

Peux-tu m’entendre ? Peux-tu me voir ? Peux-tu me sentir ?

Final Chorus (softer)
Si le pasteur d’Arcadie
Faisoit un droit jugement…

Then he’d set the fruit down gently

And never ask for it again.
Celle-ci, quoy qu’on l’envie—
She already holds the present.
And I am only the one
Who finally learned to give it.

M’entends-tu ? Me vois-tu ? Me sens-tu ? (whispers)
